Hi everyone!


I am Angelina Silvestri, a Los Angeles based designer and creative strategist who thinks like an environmentalist.


In my garden, I have learned that healthy plants need to be understood in the context of their entire ecosystem. Brands are not too different. My work is patient, holistic, and always a little messy.


My design career started with a BS in Architecture, which gave me a foundation in spatial thinking. I went on to work as a design strategist, working with large organizations on how they could enact real social change. Right now, I'm finishing my master's at USC, taking classes in business, design, and computer science. My current academic course load is about refining my creative process to be as multidisciplinary as the problems I want to solve.


If you're working on something you believe in, I'd love to hear from you. I'm always happy to share ideas, trade stories, or just chat about the process of building things that matter.
