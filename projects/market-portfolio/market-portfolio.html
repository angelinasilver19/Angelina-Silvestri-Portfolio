Process

How it
was made

This portfolio started with a single question: what would a website feel like if it was designed like an environment?

When I thought about what kind of world to build, the farmer's market felt like an obvious choice. It's a place I return to as often as I can. It's communal, abundant, and alive. I care deeply about our ecology and the natural world; it shapes every decision I make as a designer, a consumer, and a person.

The market stalls are a nod to my achitecture background: structure as a way of organizing ideas, not just housing them. In school, drawing and sketching were my favorite parts of the design process.

The illustration was drawn entirely by hand in Procreate. No AI, no generated assets. The interactivity was coded in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with AI assistance.

This is the first website I've built that feels like a world. It won't be the last.

On web environments

A website is an environment. You're asking the user to step outside the noise of the internet and into the world you've created.

Most portfolio sites are filing cabinets. Organized, legible, forgettable. This one is an attempt at something different; a space that reflects personality before it reflects credentials.

Angelina Silvestri — 2026
