Overview

Brand Identity Study

The line "You will find yourself putting this on everything" is doing more than describing the product; it predicts a behavior.

This campaign proposal takes that prediction seriously. If Fly By Jing claims it goes on everything, then the campaign's job is to find out where "everything" ends.

Each food subject is rendered as a hyperrealist oil painting. Every object is given the full fine-art treatment regardless of how absurd the subject matter. The background is a single flat field drawn from the brand's existing color palette. The jagged circle is lifted directly from Fly By Jing's packaging.

Process

How it
was made

This project started personally. I've been a Fly By Jing consumer for years. I spent time on their website and researching past Day Job campaigns to understand the brand's visual identity.

The workflow moved in loops. I hand-sketched food subjects in Procreate to establish form and composition, then brought those sketches into Flora.ai to generate the hyperrealist oil painting treatment and add the chili oil drizzle. Raw files came back into Procreate for color correction. I deliberately desaturated the food so the chili oil reads as the most important element in the hierarchy. Then I went into Figma to build the graphic system: typography, color blocking, shapes.

Once the Figma mockups were complete, I fed them back into Flora.ai to generate the OOH deliverables, billboards, wild postings, social grids, as client-ready renders. The final assets are AI-assisted, but every creative decision was made by hand first.
